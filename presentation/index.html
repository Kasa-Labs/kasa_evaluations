How we score

7 rubric criteria per output

Accuracy
Only facts from the transcript
Completeness
Captures all discussed topics
Drug Marking
{{drug:name}} tags correct
Condition Marking
{{condition:name}} tags correct
No Hallucination
Nothing invented or assumed
Formatting
Bold headers, bullet structure
Clinical Quality
Reads like a real clinical note, proper medical terminology
